# Closing-wall death reference research

Investigated 2026-10-28 for an original Blast Circuit animation. Scope is
**Bomberman 64 (1997)**, not Bomberman Hero or The Second Attack. Downloaded
footage and extracted contact sheets stay outside git; the PR screenshots show
our own game.

unknown hardware/emulator settings. Source timestamps are recording time, not
recovered simulation ticks. The contact sheets establish visible staging and
transitions, not collision rules or input intent. The existing

Sudden Death option and contemporary press-wall illustrations.

## Decision and limits

The recordings support an inward physical hazard and a quick, readable exit
with a white puff. They do **not** establish a particular accordion flatten,
original compression ratio, exact duration, or a universal no-fire rule for
every wall death. Searches for a dedicated crush-death animation did not yield
a cleaner isolated example in the material inspected.

Blast Circuit should give its existing authoritative `columns` death event a
separate physical gag: squeeze the original colored toy across the closing
direction, splay its gloves and boots, briefly hold the flattened silhouette,
then disappear in dust. This squash/flatten pose and its timing are **authored
adaptations**, not a claim to reproduce an observed original animation.
Keep blast burnout and water exits distinct, retain the current living model's
proportions, and change presentation only. Review both a close view of the gag
and a real closing-wall elimination under the ordinary battle camera; walls
must not hide the entire animation.
