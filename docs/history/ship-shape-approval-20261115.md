## Bounded corrections to fold into finishing

- **Dark slit:** there is a small black gap on the stern gallery's outer panel, at about (265, 420) in every battle frame. Close it.
- **Bow definition:** the bow is a large smooth blank with a soft saddle behind the stem, and in the oblique view it reads slightly like a draped sheet. Give it a crisp cap rail along its edge and a raised centreline ridge to the stem. This defines the existing form and is not new decoration.

## Final colour, material and life pass

- **Hull:** deep navy topsides, cream wale, a red-oxide bottom showing at the boot-top line.
- **Deck:** honey planks with peg dots.
- **Trim:** keep the teal gunwales with brass caps.
- **Bow:** planked seams converging on the stem, with the cap rail in cream.
- **Castle panels:** darker timber with two or three chunky inset panels each.
- **Taffrail:** inset panels and one stern lantern, warm at Night.
- **Details:** keep everything at 3 px or more at battle scale.
- **Sea:** darker and choppier than the lagoon stages, with a foam line riding the hull that moves with the roll, a small bow wave and restrained whitecaps.
- **Life:** a sail shadow sweeping the deck with the roll, the lantern swinging, one pennant, a couple of gulls.
- **Barrels:** bring them back unchanged.
