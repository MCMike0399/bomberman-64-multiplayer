## New reference insights

1. **An arena is a thick-walled tray sitting on a surface that contrasts with it in value and material.** The wood desk, tatami, turf and rock under the boards in [Labyrinth](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r/s/img/ss_battle08_l.jpg), [White Cross](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r/s/img/ss_battle09_l.jpg), [Junkyard](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r/s/img/ss_battle07_l.jpg) and [Panic Factory](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r/s/img/ss_battle16_l.jpg) are never the same value as the wall. This directly indicts the current beige Greedy Trap surround and the white-on-white Blizzard Low.
2. **Ice identity comes from thick translucent blocks with bevels against a pale matte floor**, not from saturated blue paint (White Cross). The grotto ice should be pale, thick and bevelled.
3. **A landform frame is one continuous slab with a deep dark undercut** ([editor 3](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r2/s/img/castle/original_en_3.jpg)) or a continuous canyon wall on several sides ([editor 4](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r2/s/img/castle/original_en_4.jpg)). It is not separate knolls. This changes my Field of Grass idea.
4. **Structural framing reads through fat tubes with chunky round junction caps** ([Grand Prix](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r2/s/img/battle/ss_en_gp_crystal.jpg)). That suits the Lost at Sea pontoon and the Gutter gantry seats. Take the scale, not the part count.
5. **One glowing display in a dark violet room** ([planet display](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r2/s/img/character/ss_story_2.jpg)): one hero globe, everything else subordinate. **Big round portholes and curved warm fittings** ([ship interior](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r2/s/img/character/ss_story_1.jpg)) make machinery friendly.
6. [Magnetic Sphere](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r/s/img/ss_battle04_l.jpg) adds nothing new beyond confirming rounded hard blocks and point 1.

## Final design per map

**Blizzard, "Glacier Lantern Grotto" (substantial).**
- **Form:** one continuous carved body. A rounded snow shoulder grows out of the berm; an open vault passes through it, with thick, pale, bevelled ice visible only on the vault's inner faces and as an undercut band.
- **Joins:** delete the separate shelf and the leg posts. The source diagnosis is that the shelf intersects the lower 0.485 world units of the legs and one rotated post overhangs the shelf. The fix is that legs and footing are the same mesh, or meet at a modelled fillet above a shared base, with no part ending inside another and no rotated free post. Verify from behind and below, not only from the battle camera.
- **Details:** 3–4 fat icicles seated with collars on the lip. One chunky tilted crystal on the crown, in the cone family. Amber lantern inside the vault.
- **Life:** the penguin shelter and pond move to the lee of the grotto (absorbing the igloo). The existing pennant and spindrift follow accepted wind.
- **Surround:** broad drifts with blue shadow sides and a darker far ring, so the white tray sits on a contrasting ground.
- **Medium:** same forms, simpler frost, fewer flakes. **Low:** full silhouette, blue/white split, emissive lantern, pennant, one penguin.

**Field of Grass, "Root-Hollow Meadow" (substantial).**
- **Form:** continue the front cutaway round both sides as one loam bank mass with a thick, rounded, slumped turf lip and a dark undercut beneath it. The actual slope is unchanged.
- **Material:** paths return to warm loam, so path and bank face read as one earth. Calm mid-green meadow beyond. Moss caps on the standing stones.
- **Life:** one gust shared by seed heads and grass. Hay shelter and stile with lamp. The burrow is optional.
- **Medium:** same. **Low:** bank mass, lip and concealment intact; one cheap sway.

**Greedy Trap, "Reservoir Counting House" (substantial).**
- **Form:** the existing canals join into one calm reservoir ring, using the current water feature, so cream coping sits against teal instead of beige.
- **Landmark:** one counting house with a large round float-gauge window; the others stay plain and smaller. Drop the round tree trays.
- **Life:** the waterwheel on a single slow rhythm, plus two or three floats. Nothing moves on the reward floor.
- **Medium:** same water as now. **Low:** opaque teal, wheel turning, emissive gauge window.

**Circuit Garden, "Harbour Bombwright Bench" (substantial).**
- **Form:** cream worktop with broad plank or slab seams, on a varnished timber body with a rounded teal fender rail and four chunky brass corner fittings.
- **Readability:** teal stair nosings. Keep the range inlays. The target raft becomes a core object.
- **Life:** post pennants and raft bob on one rhythm; one gull.
- **Remove:** the palm islet.
- **Fix:** shadow corrugation and front-edge aliasing first.
- **Low:** body, rail, inlays, raft, emissive post lamps.

**Top Rules, "Moon-Clock Keep" (moderate).**
- **Form:** a layered rounded dark-violet crag with a deep undercut. One broad brass meridian ring is seated in it, and the existing arms spring from the ring.
- **Hierarchy:** one hero moon, largest and brightest; the other globes smaller and duller. Keep one mini-island at most.
- **Light:** warm observatory windows.
- **Life:** slow orbit on a pausable clock, not tied to any hazard.
- **Low:** crag, ring, hero moon, turrets, emissive windows.

**Rock Garden, "Roundstone Orchard" (moderate).**
- **Form:** columns become warm carved stones with moss shoulders, same footprints. Boulders in two tones and two sizes. Coping about 15% calmer. Standard framing restored.
- **Landmark:** the windmill, plus one bowed fruit branch over the north wall from outside.
- **Life:** mill and branch on a shared breeze. No bee, no petals.
- **Remove:** flower-bed discs.
- **Low:** mill silhouette with rotation, branch mass, two-tone stones, dark mown perimeter.

**In the Gutter, "Copper Lock Fishworks" (moderate).**
- **Form:** one gantry becomes the active lockhouse: a large round pressure-dial porthole, visible gear seats with chunky caps, and a connected culvert stub. The other gantry stays quiet. No hut.
- **Life:** vane, gears and short wakes follow accepted current, including reversals.
- **Water:** unchanged on Medium and High. Fix the pylon reflection residue.
- **Low:** opaque jade, contact foam, vane and gear cue.

**Lost at Sea, "Glass Buoy Observatory" (moderate).**
- **Form:** a fat tube pontoon with round orange-capped corner junctions under a thicker rounded rim lip.
- **Under the glass:** a wreck rib group under one quarter, present from the start as real geometry, with one slow manta. Existing Medium refraction is kept.
- **Life:** one leaning bell buoy; beacon pulse.
- **Low:** pontoon, lit beacons, pane edges, wide sheens, painted subsurface shadow.

**Sunken Temple, "Returning Tide" (finishing).**
- The gateway islet becomes a chunky sun-disc shrine echoing the frieze.
- One lotus ruin replaces the second palm islet.
- One copper tide float rides the accepted water plane.
- Warm Night stone.
- **Low:** shrine and float retained.

**Up & Down, "Tideglass Beacon" (finishing).**
- Soft sweeping beam.
- One existing sea stack reshaped into an arch with a turf cap. No puffin nest.
- Bell buoy.
- Fix the stack shading.
- **Low:** lighthouse, arch, slow beacon accent.

**Sea Sick, "Tubby Chart Cutter" (finishing only).**
- Masthead pennant and thin waterline foam.
- At most one small rounded figurehead.
- Nothing else.

## Cross-map principles the new sources support

- **The surround must differ from the arena wall in value and material.** Check with the 320 px grayscale thumbnail.
- **One body:** landmarks grow from the base mass with modelled joins and a visible undercut or shadow; nothing free-floating.
- **One hero accent per map.** Supporting objects are smaller and duller.
- **Fat tubes, big round windows and chunky caps** are the shared machine vocabulary across Gutter, Lost at Sea, Greedy Trap and Circuit Garden.
