## How to keep this a coherent game

Use broad silhouettes and three dominant material families per map; one focal vignette and one environmental cause. Keep decorative creatures outside combat and avoid motion resembling a bomb, pickup, target or hazard. Retain warm motivated Night accents and dark/light separation beneath real bombs. High should establish mass, readable contact and deliberate composition before fine effects; lower presets inherit those same masses, palette and accepted-state motion cues.

For review, use matched seed64 High openings/combat/Night, a body view and six-to-eight real motion frames. Then compare Medium/Low identity and 320px grayscale readability. Check the miniature and eight-player HUD clearance. Current Fable/source captures decide adoption; reference appeal and changing node transforms do not establish visual success. No game source, builds, GPU session, host or release operation was changed by this research.
