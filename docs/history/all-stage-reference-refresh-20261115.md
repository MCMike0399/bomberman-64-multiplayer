# All-stage Bomberman reference refresh

Researched 2026-11-15 for a fresh review of Blast Circuit's ten battle arenas
and Circuit Garden. This extends the [stage-fidelity review](../stage-fidelity-research.md),
[living-worlds references](../living-stage-worlds-research.md),
[scenery research](../stage-scenery.md) and
[2026-11-15 stage research](stage-expansion-research-20261115.md).
It adds first-party contemporary imagery and revisits the publisher scans;
it does not repeat the roster/unlock investigation.

**Use this as reference evidence and review prompts.** Recommendations below
are original adaptations for the project's soft painted toy dioramas, not
claims that a current render is defective or that an original game used these
materials, animations or quality tiers. Current ordinary-camera images are the
visual truth. Reference models, textures, scans and footage are not game assets.

## Fresh imagery worth putting beside the current renders

These observations concern visible forms and colours. A still does not establish
wind, particle motion, material physics, reflection stability or timing.

- **Rounded landforms have weight.** R 2's [grey rockscape](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r2/s/img/castle/original_en_1.jpg)
  and [orange-bank example](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r2/s/img/castle/original_en_2.jpg)
  use thick scalloped edges and layered profiles. Transfer those scale relationships
  to cutaway earth and plinths; do not transfer the busy part density.
- **Theme is a material system.** [Medieval](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r/s/img/ss_battle05_l.jpg)
  combines round masonry and geometric trim; [Desert Fort](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r/s/img/ss_battle14_l.jpg)
  contrasts a patterned border with quieter warm lanes and surrounding sand.
  Original large motifs and bevels suit Blast Circuit better than copying their
  fine grime or repeating textures.
- **Keep the fight readable inside the world.** [Slippery Garden](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r/s/img/ss_battle06_l.jpg)
  separates dark foliage from pale routes; [Lagoon](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r/s/img/ss_battle10_l.jpg)
  gives its marine board a raised central mass, blue accents and coral-like cover.
  Neither image proves transparent glass or a particular traction rule.
- **Mechanical identity needs load-bearing forms.** The [industrial scene](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r/s/img/ss_story02_l.jpg)
  has substantial rails, bridge slabs and grating; the [brick machine](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r/s/img/ss_story03_l.jpg)
  reads through round towers and warm openings. Borrow construction and value
  hierarchy, not combat lasers, dense mesh patterns or attack effects as ambience.
- **Winter is more than pine props.** The [official snowy scene](https://www.konami.com/games/bomberman/r/s/img/ss_story01_l.jpg)
  has frosted blocks, bare branches and visible flakes. The original manual's
  [White Glacier spread](https://www.nintendo.com/eu/media/downloads/games_8/emanuals/nintendo_8/Manual_Nintendo64_Bomberman64_EN.pdf#page=11)
  provides the larger peak, ice shelves and hanging silhouettes.

**Adaptation** is a proposal/question for the fresh visual review, preserving the
accepted terrain, controls, hazards and camera contracts.

Warm sandstone, broad carved friezes and a dark court should dominate turquoise water. Burgundy brick, cream coping and teal paving separate it from Copper Lock's machinery/current. The project's reward floor remains visibly safe paving. A working quay/window/wheel vignette and quiet water activity should support, not compete with, the chamber and its exits. |

Deep ultramarine/violet panes, bright bevels, white enamel flotation and signal-orange beacons need a visible supporting body. The guidebook descriptions above are visual anchors, not recovered dimensions,
counts, movement constants or authorisation to add mechanics. In particular,
planetary Top Rules, a richer ship hull, Copper Lock machinery and Glass Buoy's
underwater world are authored extensions. The existing stage contracts own
flooding, current, wind, grass concealment and glass/rim traction.

## Low, Medium and High identity matrix

These are review targets within the [current rendering policy](../agents/visuals-and-animation.md),
not Nintendo/Konami preset claims. Low's 75% 3D scale makes subpixel trim especially
fragile. Medium is the everyday default and deserves its own review; High is not
a substitute for it.

| Environment | Low: core read that must remain | Medium: material and quiet life | High: additional richness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sunken Temple | Golden stepped silhouette, dark court, clear water boundary | Calm clear shallows, readable friezes and peripheral breeze | Reflections/caustics deepen the lagoon without washing out stairs |
| Rock Garden | Five columns, stone/lawn contrast, grounded enclosure | Broad moss/grass variation and connected gusts | Restrained lawn/lichen relief; no fine noise competing with bombs |
| Up & Down | Purple/green/pale hierarchy, supports and every lower route | Ceramic/stone finish and a quiet coastal vignette | Water/light depth; support strata remain stable rather than shimmer |
| Top Rules | Castle/crimson court, indigo setting and visible crag | Fully framed small orrery and slow toy motion | Lacquer and motivated coloured light; no extra foreground clutter |
| Greedy Trap | Three clear levels, exits, burgundy/cream/teal separation | Reservoir material contrast and one working quay | Stable reflections outside the safe reward floor |
| Blizzard Battle | Cone shapes, snow/ice edges and accepted weather cues | Frosted surfaces, varied peripheral silhouettes | Layered ice/light/snow accents; Night keeps player contrast |
| Circuit Garden | Lesson routes, targets/ranges and recognisable workshop body | Quiet water/cloth life away from instructions | Richer surrounding light while the lesson stays visually quiet |
| Field of Grass | Earth-bank mass, turf boundary and full gameplay concealment | Coherent breeze, broad clumps and distinct stone cover | Fine leaf/soil richness adds to the same valley silhouette |
| In the Gutter | Gate machines, bollards, vane, channel grounding and water/current read | Jade/mosaic contrast and short accepted-flow wakes | Stable real reflections and subtle wet trim, not bright hatch noise |
| Sea Sick | Full hull/wale/deck palette, seated waterline and clear cover | Timber/trim, warm lamp and perceptible slow roll | Water/reflection/material depth with identical geometry and cues |
| Lost at Sea | Supported white rim, violet glass/sheens and warm towers | Pane/bevel depth and a clear nonslip route | Subtle stable reflections and underwater depth; the floor never reads as a hole |

If an identity-defining part vanishes with `StageScenery`, move a cheaper version
of that form into the core presentation rather than disguising the loss with
bloom. This applies especially to a ship belly, lock machinery, glass flotation,
earth banks and training plinth. Low may hide optional ambience; it must retain
information needed to understand the rules.

## More life with fewer competing signals

Give each map one environmental cause and one focal vignette: wind connects
leaves/cloth, accepted current connects wakes/vane/gear, tide connects boats and
shore contact, and ship roll connects waterline/lantern/shadow. A few related
motions read as a place; evenly scattered unrelated props read as a kit.

Use slow, bounded cycles and a pausable stage clock. Verify perceived amplitude
in six to eight actual frames or a clip from the ordinary battle camera; a
changing transform is not proof that the movement reads. Authoritative weather
cues must follow accepted state on all clients. Decorative life remains outside
standing/aiming space and introduces no collision or fake walkable exits.

For every map, the fresh review should see matched seed, roster, camera and
lighting at Low/Medium/High; an ordinary battle and Night frame; an oblique body
view; and a miniature that includes its signature body seated on the display.
Use eight-player combat for battle maps and the actual one-player lesson for
Circuit Garden. Read a 320 px grayscale thumbnail as well as the full image.
Judge helmets, black bombs, pickups, aim markers and hazard warnings through a
real action, not an empty beauty shot. Existing good forms can be preserved;
these references are not a demand to rebuild every stage.

Only this research note was written for the refresh. No integration source,
build, graphical desktop, server, release or deployment was changed by it.
