# Living stage worlds: visual references

Research inspected on 2026-10-27 for the living-stage-worlds branch. The user
likes the current diorama identity and asks for more environmental life,
especially wind in the gardens, a richer celestial backdrop for Top Rules and
more varied snowy surroundings for Blizzard Battle. These are original scenery
extensions, not reconstructions of Bomberman assets or changes to arena rules.

The original manual artwork and the linked official screenshots were opened and
visually inspected; the R 2 narrative was read on Konami's page. The manual's
printed page numbers above differ from PDF page indices. A [Nintendo-hosted
English manual](https://www.nintendo.com/eu/media/downloads/games_8/emanuals/nintendo_8/Manual_Nintendo64_Bomberman64_EN.pdf)
is also available; the visual observations above use the Japanese scan. No
reference images are added to the repository or distributed with the game.

## Original adaptations for Blast Circuit

These are design proposals inferred from the references, not claims that the
original games contain these effects:

- **Rock Garden:** one wind direction with broad moving gusts across turf,
  bending tufts and a little lag between branches and leaves. Occasional
  drifting leaves and butterflies make the existing meadow feel inhabited.
  Preserve the five-column arena, paving, boulder silhouettes and warm stone.
- **Top Rules:** retain the floating fortress and dark negative space, but give
  that space a composition: a larger ringed planet, a smaller cool moon, sparse
  slowly tumbling asteroids and distant stars. Keep their paths outside the
  playable volume and avoid fast streaks that could be mistaken for attacks.
- **Blizzard Battle:** frame the ice court with a sculptural glacier or ice arch,
  hanging icicles and snow shelves; mix a few frosted trees with the pines.
  Extend sparse snowfall across the surrounding snowfield while preserving
  visibility of existing ice cones, bombs and authoritative wind/avalanche cues.
- **Greedy Trap and Circuit Garden:** echo the existing harbor life with a breeze
  through trees, reeds or pennants. Keep the water, existing town composition
  and range markings as the dominant features.

The references support coherent natural, winter and cosmic themes. They do not
justify replacing the current lacquered figures, High material tier, lighting
profiles, camera, depth of field, water reflections or simulation. Final
composition and motion must be judged in real battle-camera screenshots and
animated witnesses; this research alone is not visual approval.
